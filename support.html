Common questions

How do I get approved? Startender is invite-reviewed to keep the network high quality. Build your profile — including a verification selfie and at least four photos — and our team reviews every application, usually within 24 hours.

How do I delete my account? Open the app, go to Settings → Delete Account. Your data is removed per our Privacy Policy.

I'm a venue — how do I get on? Apply in the app and choose "Venue" at sign-up, or email us above.
